Castle of the Crimson Moon · testing tools

Labs

Workbenches for trying looks and sounds before they ship. Nothing here changes the game or your save: each lab hands its result back as a file or JSON to commit.

Backgrounds · 1

Fog Lab

The mist on the painted rooms, on the game's real 3D renderer: amount, wind, the puffs' motion, shape and lighting, the distance haze. COPY JSON gives the values back.

Enter › Voice-over · 2

VO Lab

Every take of the Old Wizard as the game plays it. Approve the keepers, flag the rest; RE-RENDER packs the verdicts for the generator.

Enter › Combat effects · 3

Particle Lab

Hit, crit and kill bursts side by side on the same blows: the shipped looks per material and the alternatives tried along the way.

Enter › Combat cards · 4

Card Lab

The game's real cards with three ideas on top: a shader behind each portrait (fog, blood, flames, embers, ether), the cards kicking and turning in 3D, and a glint sweeping the bitmap as it turns.

Enter › Portraits · 5

Art Lab

The portraits redrawn in the room paintings' style, on the real cards over any room: current beside the candidates, a line-up of all thirteen, a fight at the game's size. Approve or reject with a note; COPY JSON packs the verdicts and re-rolls for the generator.

Enter › Room paintings · 6

Background Lab

New room paintings from the developer's prompting guide (Seedream 4) through their stages: drafts, approved, in the game. Each room's candidates as tiles — approve, reject with a note, or regenerate with notes — the picked one under the real cards in a fight at the game's size, or beside one of the game's own paintings. COPY JSON packs the verdicts and re-rolls for the generator.

Enter › The world above the dungeon · 7

World Lab

The world map as the top layer: the castle you play today is one pin on the developer's painting, the rest under clouds. Clear a place and the clouds burn away around its roads. Two looks — the known world from above, and from the sky over one place — and the dive through the clouds that would lead into a place.
