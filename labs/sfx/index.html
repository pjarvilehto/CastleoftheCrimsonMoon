SFX Lab

Every sound effect the game plays, where it plays, through the game's own mixer — the trims, the random variation layers and the music ducking are the game's. MUSIC ON plays each section's bed under the sounds. Edit a clip's Volume, Pitch and Speed, listen, Approve what you want kept, and COPY JSON packs it for node tools/render-sfx.mjs --apply sfx-review.json (a pitch or speed change is rendered into a new file; a volume change is the clip's trim). Keys: Space play the current clip · A approve · M music on / off · V variation on / off · J/K or ↓/↑ next / previous · R reset the current clip's edits. Verdicts and edits are kept in this browser. Speed keeps the pitch (a time-stretch in this page; the Speed slider is off for the generated sounds, where pitch and speed are one knob).

Copied to the clipboard and downloaded as sfx-review.json. In the repo: node tools/render-sfx.mjs --apply sfx-review.json (--dry-run first shows what it would do), then ship. Or paste the JSON to the assistant.
