VO Lab

Every take of the Old Wizard, as the game plays it (levelled to the game's target). Listen through, approve the keepers, disapprove the rest and say what was wrong; RE-RENDER packs the verdicts for node tools/gen-vo.mjs --rerender. Keys: Space play the current take · A approve and move on · D disapprove · 1/2 volatility less / more · 3/4 shouty less / more · J/K or ↓/↑ next / previous · Esc stop. Verdicts are kept in this browser; a take re-rendered since you judged it shows as RE-RENDERED and wants a fresh verdict.
